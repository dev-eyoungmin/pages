1. 앱이 직접 수집하는 정보

바다밭 일기(이하 "앱")는 이용자의 개인정보를 직접 수집하지 않으며, 개발자가 운영하는 서버도 없습니다. - 회원가입과 로그인이 없습니다. - 광고가 없고, 광고 SDK나 추적 기능도 들어 있지 않습니다. - 게임 진행(날짜, 바다밭, 캔 것, 기술, 일기, 설정)은 기기 안에만 저장되며 외부로 보내지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워집니다. - 게임은 인터넷 연결 없이도 할 수 있습니다. 네트워크는 인앱 구입(정원사 권)에만 쓰입니다.

2. 인앱 구입

앱은 처음 7일을 무료로 제공하고, 그 뒤로 계속하려면 한 번 사는 "정원사 권"이 필요합니다. 결제는 Apple App Store(Android에서는 Google Play)가 처리합니다. 개발자는 이름, 결제 수단 등 결제 정보를 받지 않습니다. 구매를 확인하는 데 필요한 거래 정보만 기기 안에서 스토어로부터 받아 확인하며, 개발자에게 보내지 않습니다. 권을 가졌다는 표시는 기기에만 저장됩니다. 환불은 각 스토어의 환불 절차(Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com)를 따릅니다.

Privacy Policy (English)

Sea Garden Diary · Effective October 23, 2026

1. Information the app collects

Sea Garden Diary ("the app") does not collect personal information, and the developer runs no servers. - There is no account or sign-in. - There are no ads, and no ad SDK or tracking. - Game progress (days, sea garden, catch, skills, diary and settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the app. - The game can be played offline. The network is used only for the in-app purchase (Gardener's Pass).

2. In-app purchase

The first 7 days are free; to keep playing, you buy the "Gardener's Pass" once. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store (Google Play on Android). The developer never receives your name or payment details. Only the transaction information needed to confirm your purchase is received from the store and checked on your device, and it is not sent to the developer. Ownership of the pass is stored only on your device. Refunds follow each store's process (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com).

3. Game engine and purchase module

The app is built with the Unity engine and Unity IAP from Unity Technologies. Unity may receive non-identifying device and diagnostic information such as device model, OS version and app version. It is not linked to you and is not used for ads or tracking. The developer does not receive or keep it. See unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

4. Third parties and transfers

The developer does not share personal information with or entrust it to anyone. Payments are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

5. Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, erasure or restriction. The developer keeps no personal information, so a request is answered by confirming that. Deleting the app removes all game data on your device. Email the address below; we reply within 10 days. Responsible person: Youngmin Lee (be.onn), be.onn.corp@gmail.com

6. Children

The developer does not collect personal information from children. Children should decide on in-app purchases together with a parent or guardian; with Ask to Buy turned on, a purchase waits for approval.
